How It Works – TapScan QR Solutions

At TapScan, we make it simple for businesses in Pakistan to bridge offline customers with their online presence.

1

Select & Customize

Choose your stand design (Google Review, 4-in-1, or Cutout). Send us your high-resolution business logo, brand colors, and destination links.

2

We Program & Laser-Cut

Our technical team encodes the embedded NTAG213/215 NFC chip with your encrypted URL, generates high-contrast QR codes, and laser crafts the acrylic.

3

Plug & Play Counter Launch

Your stand arrives ready to use via Express Courier. Place it at your cashier counter or reception desk. Customers simply tap and review!
